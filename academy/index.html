Enrolment

From choosing a course to your certificate.

Four steps, with one Walnut sign-in. Enrolment and payment happen on Walnut LMS.

  1. 01

    Choose a course

    Pick a course above. Its title opens the course on Walnut LMS, with its lessons, level and price.

  2. 02

    Sign in with your Walnut account

    Press Enrol and sign in with the account you use on this site. You arrive on Walnut LMS already signed in.

  3. 03

    Pay on Walnut LMS

    Paid courses are paid for on Walnut LMS, at the price shown on the course.

  4. 04

    Learn and earn the certificate

    Work through the lessons on Walnut LMS. Complete a course that carries a certificate and it is issued to you there.
