    The Online Counselling Course

    Learn the skills behind effective education counselling.

    A self-paced certification for counsellors: guide a learner from first enquiry to an informed admission decision, using verified facts and needs-based counselling instead of a sales script.

    What online counselling involves

    Helping a learner choose, apply to and settle into an online or distance programme: understanding how Online, ODL and regular education differ, what a programme contains, and what the learner actually needs.

    Why it helps students

    A counsellor who works from verified facts rather than a sales script helps a student make an informed decision — the right programme, at a university they can trust, at a fee they understand.

    Why the detail matters

    Recognition and entitlement, eligibility, total fees, payment options, admission stages and documents: these are the questions every student asks, and the course trains you to answer them correctly.

    How the programme supports you

    Every module comes in four parts — a video lecture, reading notes, a module book and a self-check with a practice activity — so you can learn at your own pace and check what you have learned.

    Frequently asked questions

    Before you start.

    Answers from how the course and the partner platform actually work.

    Still have a question? Ask it in the form and our team replies.

    What is the Online Counselling Programme?

    A self-paced certification course for counsellors on Walnut LMS: 21 modules and 111 lessons, about 29 hours of learning in all, on how online higher education works and how to guide a learner from first enquiry to an informed admission decision. It ends with a final test and the Certified Online Programme Counsellor - Foundation certificate.

    Who can enrol?

    Anyone with a Walnut account. The course is at beginner level and assumes no prior counselling experience; it suits students, homemakers, working professionals and practising counsellors alike.

    How much can I earn?

    The earning opportunity opens once you are certified and registered as a Walnut partner. Earnings are performance-based: they follow the students you counsel and the admissions you complete, on your own schedule. The potential is ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 per month. Potential earnings depend on individual performance, and actual earnings are not guaranteed.

    Is the programme delivered online?

    Yes. Everything is on Walnut LMS: the video lectures, reading notes, module books, quizzes, practice activities and the final test. You learn at your own pace.

    How do I become a Walnut Partner?

    By completing the Online Counselling Course first. Partnership is an opportunity for certified learners: once you have passed the final test and hold the certificate, you can register as a Walnut partner — about you, whether you apply as an individual or an organisation, where you work, your experience and what you want to work on. Registration is not open from this page.

    What happens after partner registration?

    Registration is for certified learners. Our team reviews every application. Your dashboard shows its status — submitted, under review, approved or not approved — and you can edit the application while it is still under review. Once approved, we take you through onboarding and Course Finder opens to you. Walnut Leads needs no approval: anyone with a free Walnut account can sign up to it.

    What is Walnut Leads?

    Walnut’s marketplace for student-enquiry leads, at market.walnutdatatech.com. Anyone can create a free Walnut account and sign up to it. Leads are sold in packages: every package shows where its leads come from, the programme, the region and how recent they are, and each has its own minimum purchase. The leads come from Course Finder, collected with the student’s consent to be contacted. Each lead is sold to one buyer only and is never resold.

    What happens to the leads I buy?

    They are yours as soon as you buy: view them in the app or download them as a CSV. In your Walnut Leads account, View Leads lets you search, filter and export them, My Purchases lists every package you’ve bought, Lead Reports reports on them, and Wallet & Billing shows your balance and spending. Walnut Leads does not run follow-ups, assign leads or keep a pipeline: the counselling is yours.

    What is Course Finder?

    Walnut’s university course finder: pick a country and a programme level, refine by stream or university, and open the programmes with their details and fees where the university provides them. Part B of the course teaches how to use it to build a shortlist for a learner.

    Is partner approval required?

    For partner registration and Course Finder, yes: they open only after Walnut approves your application, never automatically on registration. Walnut Leads is different: anyone can create a free Walnut account and sign up to it.

    Are there charges for purchasing leads?

    Yes: you pay per lead. Your Walnut team sets up your wallet, and you then buy leads from it; there is no online payment. Each package has its own minimum purchase, and prices are set in the marketplace itself, not on this page. If there is a problem with a purchase, tell us through Help & feedback; approved refunds are credited back to your wallet.

    Can I use my existing Walnut account for the course and the partner platform?

    Yes. One Walnut account signs you in here, on Walnut LMS and in the partner applications. A learner account can apply as a partner later on the same account. A university account is used for the university only and cannot be combined with a partner or learner account.
