On-Screen Evaluation

Mark scanned answer sheets on screen, step by step, with AI suggestions you control.

Who it’s for Exam cells, evaluators and moderators in schools and colleges.

  • Upload question papers, marking schemes and scanned answer sheets, then assign them to evaluators.
  • Award marks for each step of the marking scheme, with ticks, crosses and comments on the page.
  • AI suggests step marks with a reason; faculty accept or override every one.
  • Moderation, re-evaluation requests and outcome-wise results, published in one click.

Learning Improvement Plans

Turn results into a personal improvement plan for every student and teacher.

Who it’s for Mentors, class teachers, principals and heads of department.

  • Plans start from each learner’s weakest learning outcomes, with a baseline and a target.
  • Suggested goals, practice and content, with mentor check-ins and progress tracking.
  • Progress updates by itself when new results are published.
  • Professional plans for faculty, linked to teacher development.

Pedagogy Engine

Design CBSE-aligned lessons with proven teaching models and track class progress.

Who it’s for School teachers, content designers and curriculum heads.

  • A curriculum map of NCERT chapters and learning outcomes, with coverage gaps highlighted.
  • A lesson designer built on 5E, gradual release and experiential models, with an AI first draft.
  • A shared content library, with review and approval by the curriculum head.
  • Assign lessons to a class; students learn step by step and parents follow progress.

Peer Learning

Form pairs and study groups from real outcome data, then run and measure sessions.

Who it’s for Teachers who facilitate, students and peer tutors.

  • Suggested pairs and groups that match strong and developing learners on the same outcomes.
  • Sessions with agendas, attendance and shared tasks.
  • Peer feedback after every session.
  • Impact reports that show mastery before and after.

Andragogy Engine

Design self-directed courses for college students, working professionals and teachers.

Who it’s for Colleges, faculty development programmes and individual learners.

  • Course design around adult-learning principles, with a completeness check.
  • Module templates for experiential, problem-based, case and flipped learning.
  • Learners set their own goals, choose their path and keep a reflection journal.
  • Faculty review reflections and see where each learner needs support.

Walnut Learning Suite

How the apps connect

Every app sits behind one portal, and every person signs in once to see the apps their institution has, with the right role in each. The apps share one language, the learning outcome. A mark on a step of an answer sheet, a lesson, an improvement goal and a peer group are all tied to the same outcomes. When results are published in On-Screen Evaluation, improvement plans update their progress, peer groups can be re-formed and teachers see which outcomes need a new lesson, with no copying between systems. Each app also works fully on its own, so an institution can start with one and add the others later.
