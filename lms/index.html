Who it is for

Built for universities, schools and institutions.

Each brings a different problem to a learning platform. Choose yours.

The problem

Online and distance programmes have to satisfy UGC and DEB norms: four-quadrant content for every course, proctored examinations, records for the regulators and the Academic Bank of Credit, and fee collection at scale, often across several systems that do not talk to each other.

What Walnut LMS does

Walnut LMS structures every course in the four quadrants, runs examinations through its own examination module with AI and human proctoring, keeps admissions, fees, loans and examination data on one student record, and produces the internal examination, verification and finance reports your office is asked for.

  • UGC four-quadrant content for every course
  • Proctored examinations with Aadhaar identity checks
  • Programme, semester, session and batch structure
  • Internal examination, verification and finance reports

The problem

Teaching, marking and improvement live in separate places. Marks are entered by hand, a result rarely changes the next lesson, and parents see little of how their child is doing.

What Walnut LMS does

The Walnut Learning Suite inside the LMS closes that loop. Answer sheets are marked on screen with AI assistance, every result feeds a learning improvement plan, lessons are designed on the NCERT curriculum map, and peer groups form around the outcomes a class finds hard.

  • On-screen evaluation of scanned answer sheets
  • An improvement plan for every student and teacher
  • CBSE-aligned lesson design with the Pedagogy Engine
  • Attendance, assignments and progress that parents can follow

The problem

Many batches, many faculty, study material that nobody formally approves, and learners who need skills they can show an employer.

What Walnut LMS does

Walnut LMS gives each batch its own courses and faculty, routes study material through a faculty approval workflow, lets adult learners set their own goals with the Andragogy Engine, tracks self-assessment attempts and study hours, and issues certificates that anyone can verify by number.

  • Batch management with faculty assigned per course
  • Content approval by session, batch, programme, semester and subject
  • Self-directed courses with the Andragogy Engine
  • Certificates verified on Walnut LMS

How it is different

An LMS that follows the UGC four-quadrant approach.

Most learning platforms store files and quizzes. Walnut LMS organises every course the way the UGC e-learning model expects, and carries examinations, marking and improvement inside the same system.

Quadrant 1

e-Tutorial

Audio and video lectures, animations, virtual labs and simulations, with full video transcriptions.

Quadrant 2

e-Content

Self-instructional reading material, e-books, PDFs, illustrations, case studies, presentations and supplementary documents.

Quadrant 3

Web resources and discussion

Curated web links, open-source content, historical overviews and further articles, with discussion spaces for peer interaction and doubt clearing.

Quadrant 4

Self-assessment

MCQs, quizzes and problem sets with solutions, assignments, FAQs and feedback that clears common misconceptions.

Proctored examinations

Examinations run inside the LMS, not beside it.

Walnut’s examination module takes a paper from the question bank to the published result, and Walnut’s own proctors run it live. Every candidate is on one console with camera, screen and microphone; AI flags what it sees and a trained proctor decides; candidates get a guided exam screen with a reason for every answer. Conducted as per prevailing UGC Regulations.

AI proctoringHuman proctoringAI and human together

01Live monitoring

Every candidate, on one console.

  • Live counts Assigned, connected, not connected, submitted, warnings and pending access, at the top of the console.
  • Candidate wall A live camera tile for every connected candidate. A tile turns dark when a camera is off and a Break label shows who is paused.
  • Assigned roster Each candidate’s status and exam controls, with a one-click Resume Exam for a paused candidate.
  • Camera, screen and mic Select a candidate to open the session: the webcam on the left, the entire screen on the right, and the microphone, all live, so the proctor sees exactly what they are doing.

02Integrity, automatically

AI flags; proctors decide.

  • Proctoring alerts Another person detected, candidate not present, left the exam window, looking away from the screen. Each is flagged to the proctor and shown to the candidate with the reason and a running warning count.
  • Integrity trust score A live score out of 100 with every deduction listed: face not consistently visible, left the exam window and for how long. Deductions are proportionate; a small issue costs a little, nothing is an automatic penalty.
  • Violation log Every event time-stamped with a snapshot, kept for review.
  • Review threshold When the score falls below the threshold the session is marked for review. A proctor makes the final decision, never the AI alone.

03Proctor controls

Time, breaks, access and resumes.

  • Add minutes, break, terminate For every candidate in the roster: extra minutes for genuine delays, a Break that locks the exam screen while monitoring stays connected, and Terminate in serious cases.
  • Manual access requests A candidate who cannot enter automatically asks the proctor, who checks the camera and chooses Approve or Reject.
  • Resume with a remark A session closed for a violation can be resumed if the proctor finds it justified. Every resume needs a written reason, so there is an audit trail of who resumed which exam and why.
  • Status at a glance Not started, in process, on break, submitted, closed due to violation.

04Talking to students

Private chat, in real time.

  • One private chat per attempt Proctors and candidates talk without disturbing the rest of the batch.
  • Clear, polite guidance “Dear student, kindly focus on camera.” “Speaking in the exam is not allowed.”
  • Help with problems “Kindly use a stable internet connection and re-login.” Access and connection issues are sorted in the chat.
  • Questions answered Students can ask about their score and their warnings, and get an answer during the exam.

05The student’s exam

What candidates see.

  • A guided exam screen Sections for multiple-choice and descriptive answers with progress, the time left and the student helpline at the top, and a small self view so candidates know the session is recorded.
  • A reason for every answer One question at a time. Each multiple-choice answer comes with a short written reason, which helps confirm genuine understanding.
  • Question palette Answered, marked for review and not visited, colour-coded by section.
  • Descriptive answers A rich-text editor for long answers, with an optional PDF or photo of working uploaded within a short time window.
