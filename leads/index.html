Walnut Leads

Student enquiries for the counsellors who help them choose.

Walnut Leads is Walnut’s marketplace for student-enquiry leads. Education counsellors, agents and consultants buy leads in packages, chosen by programme, region and freshness, and download them straight away. The leads come from Course Finder, collected with the student’s consent to be contacted.

Anyone can create a free Walnut account and sign up to Walnut Leads. Your Walnut team sets up your wallet; you then buy leads from it, paying per lead.

How it works

From sign-up to your first leads.

Four steps, all in Walnut Leads.

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Sign up with your email: Walnut’s one login sends you a one-time code, with no password to remember.

  2. 02

    Get your wallet set up

    Your Walnut team sets up your wallet; you then buy leads from it, paying per lead.

  3. 03

    Choose a package

    Every package shows where its leads come from, the programme, the region and how recent they are, so you know what you’re buying. Each package has its own minimum purchase.

  4. 04

    Download your leads

    Leads are yours as soon as you buy: view them in the app or download them as a CSV.

Your account

Everything you buy, in one place.

What you’ll find once you’re signed in to Walnut Leads.

  1. 01

    Lead Marketplace

    Every package on sale, with filters for lead source, programme, region, quality and freshness.

  2. 02

    View Leads

    Search and filter the leads you’ve bought, and export them as CSV.

  3. 03

    My Purchases

    Every package you’ve bought, in one list.

  4. 04

    Lead Reports

    Reports on the leads you’ve bought.

  5. 05

    Wallet & Billing

    Your wallet balance and what you’ve spent from it.

  6. 06

    Help & feedback

    Ask a question or report a problem through Walnut’s help desk.

Good to know

How Walnut Leads works for you.

  1. 01

    Sold once

    Each lead is sold to one buyer only and is never resold.

  2. 02

    Know what you’re buying

    Every package shows where its leads come from, the programme, the region and how recent they are.

  3. 03

    With consent

    Leads come from Course Finder. Collected with the student’s consent to be contacted.

  4. 04

    Yours straight away

    Leads are yours as soon as you buy: view them in the app or download them as a CSV.

  5. 05

    Problem with a purchase?

    Tell us through Help & feedback and we’ll look into it. Approved refunds are credited back to your wallet.
