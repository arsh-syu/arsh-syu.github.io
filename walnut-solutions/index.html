    Who it’s for

    The problem you bring, and what Walnut does about it.

    The problem

    Online and distance programmes have to satisfy UGC and DEB norms: four-quadrant content for every course, proctored examinations, records for the regulators and the Academic Bank of Credit, and fee collection at scale, often across several systems that do not talk to each other.

    What Walnut does

    Walnut LMS structures every course in the four quadrants, runs examinations through its own examination module with AI and human proctoring, keeps admissions, fees, loans and examination data on one student record, and produces the internal examination, verification and finance reports your office is asked for.

    • UGC four-quadrant content for every course
    • Proctored examinations with Aadhaar identity checks
    • Programme, semester, session and batch structure
    • Internal examination, verification and finance reports

    The problem

    Teaching, marking and improvement live in separate places. Marks are entered by hand, a result rarely changes the next lesson, and parents see little of how their child is doing.

    What Walnut does

    The Walnut Learning Suite inside the LMS closes that loop. Answer sheets are marked on screen with AI assistance, every result feeds a learning improvement plan, lessons are designed on the NCERT curriculum map, and peer groups form around the outcomes a class finds hard.

    • On-screen evaluation of scanned answer sheets
    • An improvement plan for every student and teacher
    • CBSE-aligned lesson design with the Pedagogy Engine
    • Attendance, assignments and progress that parents can follow

    The problem

    Many batches, many faculty, study material that nobody formally approves, and learners who need skills they can show an employer.

    What Walnut does

    Walnut LMS gives each batch its own courses and faculty, routes study material through a faculty approval workflow, lets adult learners set their own goals with the Andragogy Engine, tracks self-assessment attempts and study hours, and issues certificates that anyone can verify by number.

    • Batch management with faculty assigned per course
    • Content approval by session, batch, programme, semester and subject
    • Self-directed courses with the Andragogy Engine
    • Certificates verified on Walnut LMS

    Walnut LMS

    One learning platform, from the first lecture to the final result.

    Walnut LMS runs online and blended programmes for universities, schools and institutions, with secure proctored examinations and the Walnut Learning Suite built in.

    Every course in the UGC four quadrants

    1. Quadrant 1

      e-Tutorial

      Audio and video lectures, animations, virtual labs and simulations, with full video transcriptions.

    2. Quadrant 2

      e-Content

      Self-instructional reading material, e-books, PDFs, illustrations, case studies, presentations and supplementary documents.

    3. Quadrant 3

      Web resources and discussion

      Curated web links, open-source content, historical overviews and further articles, with discussion spaces for peer interaction and doubt clearing.

    4. Quadrant 4

      Self-assessment

      MCQs, quizzes and problem sets with solutions, assignments, FAQs and feedback that clears common misconceptions.

    The Walnut Exam Module, live

    Secure, live-proctored online exams.

    How Walnut runs and monitors every online examination, from the candidate wall to the final answer. This is the module as it runs today for a state university’s online and distance programmes: trained proctors, automatic integrity checks and a guided exam screen, operated end to end by Walnut.

    Every candidate, on one console.

    Live counts
    Assigned, connected, not connected, submitted, warnings and pending access, at the top of the console.
    Candidate wall
    A live camera tile for every connected candidate. A tile turns dark when a camera is off and a Break label shows who is paused.
    Assigned roster
    Each candidate’s status and exam controls, with a one-click Resume Exam for a paused candidate.
    Camera, screen and mic
    Select a candidate to open the session: the webcam on the left, the entire screen on the right, and the microphone, all live, so the proctor sees exactly what they are doing.

    AI flags; proctors decide.

    Proctoring alerts
    Another person detected, candidate not present, left the exam window, looking away from the screen. Each is flagged to the proctor and shown to the candidate with the reason and a running warning count.
    Integrity trust score
    A live score out of 100 with every deduction listed: face not consistently visible, left the exam window and for how long. Deductions are proportionate; a small issue costs a little, nothing is an automatic penalty.
    Violation log
    Every event time-stamped with a snapshot, kept for review.
    Review threshold
    When the score falls below the threshold the session is marked for review. A proctor makes the final decision, never the AI alone.

    Time, breaks, access and resumes.

    Add minutes, break, terminate
    For every candidate in the roster: extra minutes for genuine delays, a Break that locks the exam screen while monitoring stays connected, and Terminate in serious cases.
    Manual access requests
    A candidate who cannot enter automatically asks the proctor, who checks the camera and chooses Approve or Reject.
    Resume with a remark
    A session closed for a violation can be resumed if the proctor finds it justified. Every resume needs a written reason, so there is an audit trail of who resumed which exam and why.
    Status at a glance
    Not started, in process, on break, submitted, closed due to violation.

    Private chat, in real time.

    One private chat per attempt
    Proctors and candidates talk without disturbing the rest of the batch.
    Clear, polite guidance
    “Dear student, kindly focus on camera.” “Speaking in the exam is not allowed.”
    Help with problems
    “Kindly use a stable internet connection and re-login.” Access and connection issues are sorted in the chat.
    Questions answered
    Students can ask about their score and their warnings, and get an answer during the exam.

    What candidates see.

    A guided exam screen
    Sections for multiple-choice and descriptive answers with progress, the time left and the student helpline at the top, and a small self view so candidates know the session is recorded.
    A reason for every answer
    One question at a time. Each multiple-choice answer comes with a short written reason, which helps confirm genuine understanding.
    Question palette
    Answered, marked for review and not visited, colour-coded by section.
    Descriptive answers
    A rich-text editor for long answers, with an optional PDF or photo of working uploaded within a short time window.

    Behind the scenes

    Trained proctors supervise every live batch, each with an assigned set of candidates, while a shared wall display gives the whole team one view of the examination. AI flags what it sees; a person decides.

    What the examination module includes

    • AI, human or combined proctoring, chosen per examination
    • Candidate identity through a live Aadhaar check and face match
    • Questions generated from the syllabus with AI or added manually, in nine types
    • Two-step verification before any paper is released
    • Encrypted questions and exam sets
    • Analytics of the integrity score
    • Recordings and screenshots of the students’ activity during the exam
    • An integrity score that flags absence, face movement and multiple voices
    • Evaluation queue and answer-key unlock after the examination
    • Each question routed to the faculty member who authored it
    • Results on the student’s record in Walnut LMS
    • Conducted as per prevailing UGC Regulations

    Frequently asked questions

    Before you get in touch.

    Something we haven’t answered? Ask it in the form and a consultant replies.

    What does Walnut Data Tech do for a university?

    Technology and services to launch, run and grow online programmes: 11 service areas in four stages. Set up (infrastructure, content and Walnut LMS), enrol (marketing, counselling and admissions), run (proctored examinations, student support, reporting and automation) and outcomes (apprenticeship and placement assistance).

    Do we have to take everything?

    No. You can start from the complete stack or choose only the service areas you need. The five apps of the Walnut Learning Suite can also be taken one at a time, as a bundle or as the complete suite.

    How is it priced?

    In one of two ways: revenue sharing, where each service is priced as a percentage of programme revenue, or a one-time payment, where each service has a fixed price agreed up front. The price of each service is shared in your proposal.

    Does Walnut LMS follow the UGC’s requirements?

    Every course is organised in the UGC four quadrants: e-tutorial, e-content, web resources and discussion, and self-assessment. Digital content is delivered through the LMS and mobile app in accordance with UGC Regulations, and proctored examinations are conducted as per prevailing UGC Regulations.

    How are online examinations proctored?

    With AI proctoring, human proctoring, or both together, chosen for each examination. Trained proctors supervise every live batch, each with an assigned set of candidates. AI flags what it sees; a proctor makes the final decision, never the AI alone.

    How do you know the right person is sitting the paper?

    Through a live Aadhaar check (eKYC or document capture), a face match before entry, a verification lobby and liveness checks during the examination.

    Can questions be set from our own syllabus?

    Yes. Questions are generated from the uploaded syllabus with AI, added manually, uploaded in bulk or digitised from the University’s question banks, in nine question types, and reviewed by faculty. A paper is released only after two-step verification.

    Do you help with UGC/DEB and Academic Bank of Credit reporting?

    Yes. Walnut prepares institutional reports on admissions, fees collection, student backlogs, examination data and academic records as required by the University and regulatory authorities including UGC/DEB, and supports registration and data sharing with the Academic Bank of Credit.

    Can the LMS carry our own branding?

    Yes. A super admin runs many institutions on one platform, each with its own logo, colours, roles and settings.

    Where is our data stored?

    In accordance with the University’s requirements and mutually agreed terms.

    Do you work with schools and colleges, or only universities?

    All three. Schools use the Walnut Learning Suite inside the LMS: on-screen evaluation, improvement plans and CBSE-aligned lesson design. Colleges and institutions get batch management, a faculty approval workflow for study material, self-directed courses and certificates anyone can verify by number.

    What happens after we send the form?

    A Walnut consultant gets in touch to understand your programmes, shows you Walnut LMS with sample data from your kind of programme, and sends a proposal for the service areas you choose.
