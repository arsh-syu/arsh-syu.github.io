Counselling and admissions

Online counselling course

A self-paced certification for counsellors: guide a learner from first enquiry to an informed admission decision, using verified facts and needs-based counselling instead of a sales script.

Level
Beginner
Length
2 hours
Modules
21 modules, 111 lessons
Price
₹999
Certificate
Certified Online Programme Counsellor - Foundation

What you will learn

  • Explain Online, ODL/Distance and Regular education and the Four-Quadrant model.
  • Verify programme entitlement for an academic session, and tell recognition, accreditation and ranking apart.
  • Describe a programme and its syllabus in terms of the learner's goal.
  • Profile a learner and identify their needs before recommending a programme.
  • Compare universities on identical parameters and demonstrate the LMS.
  • Explain total fees, payment options, admission steps and documents.
