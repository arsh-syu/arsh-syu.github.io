Walnut Jobs

International jobs at leading companies, and the Walnut courses that get you ready for them.

Walnut Jobs is an international job portal for multinationals and other leading employers, joined to Walnut LMS. Every job lists the skills it needs. If a job needs a skill you don’t have yet, Walnut shows the course that teaches it. Finish it, earn the certificate, and apply with verified skills.

How it works

From a job you want to an application you’re ready for.

Four steps. The portal tells you what each role needs, and Walnut LMS teaches and certifies it.

  1. 01

    Find a role

    Search international jobs by country, skills, work mode and salary. Every job lists the skills it needs, and how well.

  2. 02

    See your skill gaps

    Your profile is matched to each role, so you see where you already fit and which skills are missing.

  3. 03

    Finish the Walnut course and earn a certificate

    Each gap points to the Walnut course that closes it. Complete the course and its certificate adds certified skills to your profile.

  4. 04

    Apply with verified skills

    Apply once the role’s must-have skills are covered and you hold any certificate the employer requires. Employers can check every Walnut certificate by its number.

For candidates

Know what each role needs, close the gap and apply ready.

  • A profile with your skills, experience, the countries you want to work in and who can see it.
  • Jobs ranked by how well you fit, with the skill gaps for each role.
  • The Walnut course that closes each gap, with its certificate.
  • Certificates employers can verify, which add certified skills to your profile.
  • Job-ready bundles that group the courses one role needs.
